# About Me

I am a software engineer and technical writer based in Bangladesh. Currently, I work at **Therap (BD) Ltd.**, where I architect accessible, enterprise-grade healthcare solutions for the US market. My journey in technology began at the **University of Rajshahi**, where I studied Computer Science and Engineering.

### Engineering & Writing

I believe that code and documentation are two sides of the same coin. As an engineer, I specialize in the **React** ecosystem, **TypeScript**, and **AWS** infrastructure. As a writer, I focus on making complex technical standards—like **WCAG 2.1** and **HIPAA compliance**—approachable and implementable for modern dev teams.

Some things I care about:

- Clean architecture & maintainable code
- Developer education and documentation
- Performance, DX, and simplicity
- Ethical and Shariah-compliant finance
- Building useful tools with minimal complexity

I spent my early career at **Bizzntek Ltd.**, moving from Intern to Associate Software Engineer, which gave me a deep appreciation for the full software development lifecycle. Today, my mission is to build a web that is inclusive by default. I regularly document my findings on manual screen-reader testing (**NVDA/JAWS**) and type-safe architecture to help other developers build more resilient products.

### Beyond the Code

When I’m not auditing components for accessibility or publishing **NPM packages**, I’m exploring the intersection of cloud-native design and user privacy. I’m a firm believer in "building in public" and sharing the milestones of my journey from a CSE student to a Software Engineer.
